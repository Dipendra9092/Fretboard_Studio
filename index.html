How to use

Choose + Scale or + Triad to highlight notes across the neck. Choose + My notes and tap any fret to draw. Selecting an existing layer brings it into focus. Select a generated layer, then use its Edit button to change the key or type. Switch Notes / Numbers globally at any time. Everything saves to this device automatically.

Practice notes

Start with C# minor pentatonic, then add an A major triad. Your pentatonic notes will fade and the A triad will become bright. Add another custom layer for fingerings, chord tones or your own colours. Switch back to any layer with a single tap.

Your session is stored locally in this browser. Use Backup to move it to another device; nothing is uploaded to a server.
